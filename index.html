System 1 AI: Why Convai Innovations' Laya Models are the Future of High-Speed Production Routing

The release of the Laya family of models by Convai Innovations marks a significant shift toward 'System 1' decision models. Unlike generative LLMs, Laya is a non-autoregressive model designed for high-speed, deterministic text classification and structured data extraction, making it ideal for production-critical tasks like email triage and guardrail enforcement.
