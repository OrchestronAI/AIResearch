Beyond Autoregression: Deploying Decision Models for High-Frequency Agentic Guardrails

This article explores the architectural shift from using large autoregressive LLMs as judges to deploying specialized, non-autoregressive 'Decision Models' (like Jev and Kev) for local agentic workflows. By leveraging local hardware like the M5 Ultra Mac Studio, developers can achieve high-performance local execution for structured, typed decisions in agentic loops.
